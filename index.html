The pitch

Why a Digital Shop?

For businesses that want to go online but do not want the cost and complexity of a full website or marketplace.

Made for the business next door.

A digital shop gives a local business a professional online presence while keeping the buying conversation simple.

  • Customers can see your products anytime.
  • Works directly from a mobile browser.
  • QR code can be displayed inside the shop.
  • Orders can come straight to WhatsApp.
  • Products, prices and categories can be updated.
  • No separate customer app is required.
